All five use their official Roblox meshes. Their longest dimensions are 100 mm. Repairs, wall thickness and joins are documented in each model's README. STL contains shapes without textures or particle effects. These are display models that can be resized in Bambu Studio.

All actual STL exports were reimported and checked for closed connected geometry, no nonmanifold edges, no degenerate triangles and positive volume. Bambu Studio slicing and physical prints remain untested. Inspect supports and sliced layers for your printer before printing.
